隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

微信待办日程（WeChat Planner）· 更新 / Updated: 2026-11-11

中文

  • 访问的 Google 数据：只申请 https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata 权限，只能读写本 App 在你 Google Drive 中的专用隐藏区（appDataFolder），看不到、也不会访问 Drive 中的其他文件。
  • 用途：电脑端把整理好的待办任务（标题、说明、截止时间、来源群名、发送人、消息时间，以及与该任务相关的群消息原文）写入该隐藏区；手机 App 读取后导入本机，随即删除云端文件。
  • 存储：待办数据只保存在你的手机本机（App 私有存储）和你自己的电脑上。安排了日期的待办会写入手机上一个单独的本地日历「微信待办」。App 会读取手机日历用于显示日程，但不修改、不上传你原有的日程。
  • 不共享：不向开发者或任何第三方服务器发送数据，不做广告、分析或跟踪，不出售或转让任何数据。
  • 删除：在 App 设置中点「停止同步」即停止访问 Drive；卸载 App 会删除本机数据；也可以在 Google 账号「第三方应用和服务」中随时撤销授权。

English

  • Google data accessed: only the drive.appdata scope. The app can read and write only its own hidden application data folder in your Google Drive and cannot see any other Drive files.
  • Purpose: a companion script on your computer writes task files (title, description, deadline, source group name, sender, message time, and the text of the related group messages) to that folder; the Android app downloads them, imports them locally, and deletes the files from Drive.
  • Storage: task data is kept only on your phone (app-private storage) and your own computer. Scheduled tasks are written to a separate local calendar on the device. The app reads your device calendars to display events but never modifies or uploads them.
  • No sharing: no data is sent to the developer or any third-party server; no ads, analytics or tracking; no data is sold or transferred.
  • Deletion: use "Stop sync" in the app settings, uninstall the app to remove local data, or revoke access at any time in your Google Account under "Third-party apps & services".

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
