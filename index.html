微信待办日程

WeChat Planner · 个人使用的安卓待办与日程工具

这个 App 把电脑上整理出的班级群任务放进手机里，让使用者自己审核：忽略、放进待办池，或安排到某天的日程；安排好的待办会写进手机日历里一个单独的「微信待办」日历，可以打勾完成，没做完的可以退回待办池。

English

WeChat Planner is a personal Android to-do and calendar app. Tasks prepared on the owner's computer are delivered to the phone through a private, app-only folder in the owner's own Google Drive (appDataFolder). On the phone the owner reviews each task, ignores it, keeps it in a backlog, or schedules it on a date; scheduled tasks are written to a separate local calendar on the device.

Google 数据的使用 / Use of Google data

仅申请 drive.appdata 权限，只能读写本 App 在 Drive 中的专用隐藏区，无法看到 Drive 里的其他文件。详见 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy。

Only the drive.appdata scope is requested. See the Privacy Policy.
